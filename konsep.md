## 2. PREMISE / STORY

### Setup:
The protagonist (age 20) returns to his stepmother's house after 2 years away at college. His father recently died under mysterious circumstances. The will states MC must live in the family home for 6 months to claim the inheritance — but the debts are massive.

Among his father's belongings, MC finds a **cursed pendant** — an artifact that grants the power of **"Influence"**: the ability to erode mental resistance, amplify suppressed desires, and eventually bend willpower entirely. The pendant feeds on sexual energy — the more MC uses it, the stronger it grows, but it also slowly corrupts HIM.

### The Dark Truth (Revealed later):
MC's father used the same pendant on Marina years ago — that's why she married him. The pendant's power is hereditary. The women in the house have latent "marks" from father's previous use — making them MORE susceptible to MC's influence than strangers would be.

### Core Tension:
- MC is broke, trapped in the house, surrounded by beautiful step-family
- The pendant whispers to him, encouraging darker actions
- Each woman has secrets that can be weaponized
- The house itself has hidden rooms/cameras (father's legacy)
- Optional rival (neighbor/coworker) threatens MC's "territory"
- MC can choose: seduce naturally, corrupt gradually, or break them completely

### Tone:
- Starts awkward/comedic (false sense of safety)
- Rapidly shifts to tension, manipulation, power dynamics
- Mid-game: full corruption/exploitation territory
- Late-game: mind-break, total submission, or twisted "love"
- NOT lighthearted — consequences are real, characters change permanently
