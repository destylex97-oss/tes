## 2. PREMISE / STORY

### Setup:
The protagonist (age 20) is forced to return to his stepmother's house after getting kicked out of college — couldn't pay tuition after his father died of illness. He's broke, bitter, and has nowhere else to go.

His stepmother Marina offers him a place to stay: "You're still family." The house is the only free roof over his head while he figures out what to do with his life.

What he didn't expect: living with three beautiful women under one roof — his stepmother, older stepsister, and younger stepsister — all of whom have their own secrets, weaknesses, and desires hidden beneath the surface.

MC is not a wizard. He's not gifted. He's just a guy who starts paying attention — watching their habits, reading their vulnerabilities, and learning how to exploit opportunities. Some call it manipulation. Some call it survival. The player decides how far to take it.

### Core Tension:
- MC is broke, has no power, no status — starts from zero
- Three beautiful women live with him, each with exploitable weaknesses
- MC must earn money (part-time jobs) while building leverage over the household
- Each woman has dirty secrets that can be discovered and weaponized
- The house has thin walls, shared spaces, and plenty of "accident" opportunities
- Optional rival characters threaten MC's progress (NTR if enabled)

### Tone:
- Starts as awkward homecoming (realistic, grounded)
- MC observes, learns routines, finds opportunities
- Gradually escalates from "accidental" peeping to deliberate manipulation
- Mid-game: active corruption, exploitation, training
- Late-game: full control, mind-break, or twisted love — depending on path
- Grounded and realistic — no magic, no bullshit excuses

### Why No Supernatural:
- MC earns every "victory" through player skill and patience
- More satisfying — you manipulated them, not a magic rock
- Characters' downfall feels earned, not hand-waved
- "Influence" = MC's growing social intelligence, not a cheat code

## 4. GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS

### 4.1 Core Loop (Daily Cycle)

```
MORNING  → Choose: Interact / Spy / Work (money) / Observe
NOON     → Choose: Interact / Errands / Spy / Gift/Item use
EVENING  → Choose: Interact / Spy / Set traps / Prepare for night
NIGHT    → Choose: Sleep / Sneak (voyeur/grope) / Night visit / Use items on target
```

Each time slot = 1 action. A full day = 4 actions.
Special: Some actions are FREE (checking phone, reviewing camera footage, planning).

### 4.2 MC Skill System (Replaces "Influence" — No Supernatural)
