## 2. PREMISE / STORY

### Setup:
The protagonist (age 20) returns to his stepmother's house after being away for 2 years (college in another city). His father recently passed away, leaving him as the only male in the household. The will states he must live in the family home for at least 6 months to inherit his father's assets.

Upon arriving, he discovers a strange artifact among his father's belongings — a pendant that grants him the power of **"Influence"**: the ability to subtly lower people's mental inhibitions and amplify their hidden desires. The power doesn't create desire from nothing — it only removes the filters people use to suppress what they already want.

### Core Conflict:
- The protagonist needs money (father's debts) and must stay in the house
- Three beautiful step-family members live with him
- The "Influence" power grows stronger with use, but overuse has consequences
- Each woman has her own secrets, desires, and vulnerabilities
- An optional NTR threat exists (a neighbor/colleague who also has eyes on the family)

### Tone:
- Starts as awkward slice-of-life comedy
- Gradually shifts to erotic tension and corruption
- Slowburn — no instant gratification, buildup matters
- Dark undertones beneath the surface (family secrets, hidden desires)

## 4. GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS

### 4.1 Core Loop (Daily Cycle)

```
MORNING  → Choose: Talk / Observe / Help around house
NOON     → Choose: Go out (work/errands) / Stay home (interact)
EVENING  → Choose: Interact / Spy / Use Influence
NIGHT    → Choose: Sleep / Sneak around / Night events (if triggered)
```

Each time slot = 1 action. A full day = 4 actions.
Week = 7 days (some events are day-specific).

### 4.2 Stat System (Per Character)

| Stat | Description | Max |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Affection** | How much she likes/trusts MC | 100 |
| **Corruption** | How far her inhibitions have fallen | 100 |
| **Lust** | Current arousal/sexual tension | 100 |
| **Obedience** | Willingness to follow MC's lead | 100 |
| **Suspicion** | How much she suspects MC of manipulation | 100 |

**Relationships between stats:**
- High Affection + Low Corruption = Romance path (slower but more scenes)
- Low Affection + High Corruption = Domination path (faster but riskier)
- High Lust = Temporary boost to all interactions
- High Suspicion = Events get blocked, potential game-over if maxed
- Obedience unlocks specific commands/requests

### 4.3 "Influence" Power System

| Level | Name | Effect | Unlock |
|-------|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | **Read** | See hidden thoughts/desires during dialogue | Start |
| 2 | **Nudge** | +5 bonus to corruption gain per interaction | Week 2 |
| 3 | **Suggest** | Unlock "suggestion" dialogue options | Week 4 |
| 4 | **Command** | Force specific actions (high suspicion risk) | Week 8 |
| 5 | **Dominate** | Full mind-influence, minimal resistance | Week 12+ |

**Risk mechanic:** Using Influence increases Suspicion. If Suspicion > 80, the character becomes wary and events lock. Must reduce suspicion through "normal" interactions.

### 4.4 Path System

**Every character has 3 approach paths:**

| Path | Method | Scene Type | Requirement |
|------|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **Love** | Build genuine affection first, corruption follows naturally | Romantic, passionate, consensual | Affection > 60 before Corruption > 40 |
| **Corruption** | Slowly erode morals through exposure, manipulation, Influence | Reluctant-to-willing, guilt-pleasure | Corruption > Affection at all times |
| **Domination** | Use power and authority to establish control | Submissive, obedient, power-dynamic | Obedience > 60, Influence Level 3+ |

**All paths lead to explicit H-scenes** — the difference is:
- How the character acts (loving vs reluctant vs submissive)
- Dialogue during scenes
- CG expressions (blushing romantic vs tearful pleasure vs blank obedience)
- Unlocked positions/acts

### 4.5 NTR System (Optional — Toggleable in Settings)

When enabled:
- A rival character exists (neighbor, Marina's coworker, Kaori's boss)
- If MC ignores a character too long, rival makes moves
- Triggers jealousy events and "rescue" scenarios
- Can lead to NTR scenes (voyeur) or NTR-prevention (MC intervenes)
- Player can toggle this OFF at game start for pure harem route

### 4.6 Harem Route

Once all three characters reach Affection 70+ OR Corruption 70+:
- Special harem events unlock
- Characters become aware of each other's relationships with MC
- Can lead to jealousy (conflict) or acceptance (threesome/group scenes)
- Final harem ending requires managing all three simultaneously

## 5. MINI-GAMES

### 5.1 Money-Making Mini-Game

**Purpose:** Earn money to buy gifts, upgrade house, pay bills (keeps sandbox engaging)

**Type:** Simple part-time job system

| Job | Mini-Game Type | Pay | Unlock |
|-----|--------------|-----|--------|
| **Delivery** | Timing-based (click at right moment) | $20-50/run | Start |
| **Freelance Writing** | Word puzzle / typing challenge | $30-80/job | Week 2 |
| **Online Trading** | Simple buy-low-sell-high | $50-200/trade | Week 4 |
| **Photography** | Point-and-click (can photograph heroines for bonus $$$) | $100+/shoot | Corruption 30+ on any char |

**Money uses:**
- Gifts (boost Affection)
- Home upgrades (new rooms = new scene locations)
- Items (alcohol, lingerie, toys — boost Corruption/Lust)
- Dates (outside events)
- Special items (hidden camera, sleeping pills — dark path tools)

### 5.2 H-Scene Mini-Games

**Purpose:** Interactive scenes, not just static CG + text

**Types:**
| Mini-Game | Description | Used In |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Rhythm/Timing** | Click in rhythm to match pace — determines "performance" | Sex scenes |
| **Stamina Bar** | Manage stamina to last longer — better performance = more affection/corruption | All H-scenes |
| **Position Selector** | Choose positions mid-scene, each has different stat bonuses | Repeatable scenes |
| **Clothing Removal** | Drag-to-remove clothing pieces in order | Foreplay scenes |
| **Resistance Meter** | For corruption path — keep pushing without triggering "stop" | Early corruption scenes |

**Performance Rating:**
- S Rank = Maximum stat gain + bonus CG unlock
- A Rank = Normal stat gain
- B Rank = Reduced stat gain
- C Rank = Minimal gain, possible negative reaction

## 7. PROGRESSION & PACING

### Week 1-2: INTRODUCTION
- Meet all characters, establish dynamics
- Learn house routines (who is where, when)
- Unlock Influence Level 1 (Read)
- First accidental ecchi moments
- Affection gain only (no corruption yet)

### Week 3-4: TENSION BUILDING
- Influence Level 2 unlocks
- First corruption opportunities appear
- Characters start showing vulnerability
- Money system activates
- First "close call" scenes (almost caught, almost kissed)

### Week 5-8: ESCALATION
- Influence Level 3 unlocks
- First explicit scenes available (for characters at Stage 3+)
- Path divergence becomes clear
- NTR threats begin (if enabled)
- Relationship dynamics shift (characters notice MC's attention)

### Week 9-12: DEEP CORRUPTION
- Influence Level 4 unlocks
- Heavy H-scenes available
- Characters fully committed to their paths
- Harem route events begin
- Major story revelations (father's secrets)

### Week 13+: ENDGAME
- Influence Level 5
- All scenes available
- Ending routes lock in
- Final confrontations (NTR resolution, family acceptance, etc.)

## 11. UNIQUE SELLING POINTS

1. **Influence system** — Not just "click through dialogue," player actively uses supernatural power with risk/reward
2. **True sandbox** — Not linear; player decides who to pursue, when, and how
3. **Multiple paths per character** — Same girl, 3 completely different experiences
4. **Slowburn that pays off** — Building tension makes scenes more satisfying
5. **Mini-games in H-scenes** — Interactive, not passive viewing
6. **Consequences** — Overusing power has real gameplay impact (suspicion, lockouts)
7. **NTR toggle** — Caters to both audiences without alienating either
8. **Harem endgame** — Reward for players who manage all relationships
